Privacy Policy for SwatGo

Last Updated: October 2026

SwatGo ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SwatGo mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our delivery application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with delivery services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and delivery addresses when you create an account or place an order.
  • Location Data: We collect precise and approximate location data from your mobile device to facilitate delivery tracking, route optimization for riders, and to show nearby shops on the map.
  • Device Information: We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) which requires us to collect device identifiers (like FCM registration tokens) to send you push notifications about order statuses.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app and our web platforms.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To enable real-time order tracking using Google Maps.
  • To send transactional notifications (order accepted, rider on the way, order delivered).
  • To calculate distance-based delivery fees.
  • To improve our services and customer support.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for database (Firestore) and authentication.
  • Google Maps API: Used for location selection and navigation.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used for push notifications.

4. WebView Technology

Our app functions as a wrapper that loads our official web applications (Customer, Merchant, Rider, Admin) hosted on GitHub Pages. Your interactions within the WebView are governed by this same privacy policy. We do not inject or collect additional data beyond what is stated in this policy.

5. Data Security

We value your trust. We use commercially acceptable means of protecting your data, including secure Firebase Firestore databases and secure server connections. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please contact us at [YOUR_EMAIL_HERE]. We will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
